Meet Sandra

The midwife behind the site

I practised as a midwife in Ghana, caring for mothers through pregnancy, birth, and the newborn days. In Ghana — as in many countries — midwifery stands as its own distinct profession. In Canada, much of that same care is carried out within nursing, which is why I'm now studying nursing here. My mission, on my YouTube channel From Womb to World and here on this site, is to help people understand midwifery itself: its branches and its topics, from antenatal care to labour and birth to the first weeks with your baby — taught plainly, warmly, and without frightening you.

Thousands of mothers watch along every week. You're welcome here, wherever you are in your journey.
